Luxe Perfume is a Palestinian family-owned business proudly rooted in St. Thomas, United States Virgin Islands. Our story is shaped by the values we grew up with: family, resilience, generosity, and welcoming every guest with genuine hospitality.

We bring those values to every customer who walks into Luxe Fragrances and Perfume World. Whether you call St. Thomas home or are visiting our islands, our family is here to help you find a fragrance that feels personal, memorable, and entirely your own.
